# Data Dictionary - Customer Sales Transactions

This data dictionary documents the fields in the Customer Sales Transactions dataset, including their descriptions, original vs. cleaned data types, business relevance, identified data quality issues, and the cleaning/transformation rules applied.

## Table of Variables

| Field Name | Type (Raw) | Type (Cleaned) | Description | Business Relevance | Injected Quality Issues | Cleaning & Transformation Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `transaction_id` | `Object` (String) | `Object` (String) | A unique alphanumeric identifier for each transaction. | Used for audit tracking, counting distinct orders, and joining with other databases (e.g., shipping, returns). | Missing values (1.29%), inconsistent formatting (e.g., lowercase `txn-10023` vs standard `TXN10023`), and duplicates. | 1. Convert all transaction IDs to uppercase.<br>2. Remove hyphens so they follow the `TXN#####` format.<br>3. Drop rows with missing `transaction_id` (nulls are not allowed for primary keys).<br>4. Drop duplicate records based on `transaction_id`. |
| `customer_id` | `Object` (String) | `Object` (String) | A unique alphanumeric identifier for each customer. | Critical for tracking customer purchase frequency, retention, and calculating Customer Lifetime Value (LTV). | Missing values (1.29%) and duplicate customer transactions (expected in transactions, but problematic if duplicate transactions exist). | 1. Drop rows with missing `customer_id` (nulls prevent linking transaction to a specific customer account).<br>2. Standardize formatting to uppercase `CUST-####`. |
| `customer_name` | `Object` (String) | `Object` (String) | Full name of the customer. | Used for personalized marketing and communications. | Inconsistent capitalization (lowercase, uppercase), leading/trailing whitespace, and missing values (0.69%). | 1. Clean whitespace using `.str.strip()`.<br>2. Standardize casing to Title Case (e.g. `John Doe`).<br>3. Impute missing names as `"Unknown Customer"` or drop them if they cannot be reconciled. |
| `customer_email` | `Object` (String) | `Object` (String) | Email address of the customer. | Primary communication channel for marketing, transactional updates, and digital receipts. | Missing values (1.69%), mixed casing, and malformed emails (e.g., domains ending in `@com` without a dot, missing `@`). | 1. Clean whitespace and convert all emails to lowercase.<br>2. Filter out or mark invalid/malformed emails (emails missing `@` or having incorrect domains like `@com` instead of `.com` or `.org`). If invalid, replace with `"invalid_email@example.com"` or drop them. |
| `date_of_birth` | `Object` (String) | `datetime64[ns]` | The customer's birthdate. | Used for customer segmentation, age group analysis, and birthday-related loyalty offers. | Inconsistent date formats (`YYYY-MM-DD`, `DD-MM-YYYY`, `MM/DD/YYYY`), and outliers (unreasonable years like 1845, future years like 2027). | 1. Parse date using Pandas `pd.to_datetime()` with `errors='coerce'` or format detection.<br>2. Filter out unrealistic dates of birth (e.g., birth year < 1920 or birth year > current year 2026) and replace with `NaT` (Not a Time) or drop.<br>3. Calculate derived feature `customer_age`. |
| `transaction_date` | `Object` (String) | `datetime64[ns]` | Date when the purchase was completed. | Essential for time-series analysis, calculating monthly revenue, sales forecasting, and seasonal trends. | Inconsistent date formats (`YYYY-MM-DD`, `DD/MM/YYYY`, `YYYY/MM/DD`), and missing values (0.79%). | 1. Standardize and parse all transaction dates to ISO format (`YYYY-MM-DD`).<br>2. Drop records with missing `transaction_date` (crucial for time-based metrics). |
| `product_category` | `Object` (String) | `Category` | The department/class of the purchased item. | Used for inventory planning, product performance analysis, and category-level recommendation engines. | Free-text variations and typos (e.g., "Elec", "electrnics", "ELECTronics", "home", "Home and Kitchen", "Kitchen", "aparel", "Clothing"). | 1. Standardize and map free-text inputs to five core categories:<br>- `Electronics`<br>- `Home & Kitchen`<br>- `Apparel`<br>- `Books`<br>- `Sports & Outdoors`<br>2. Cast as a Pandas Category type for performance. |
| `purchase_amount` | `Object` (String / Float) | `Float64` | Total dollar value of the transaction. | Core financial metric. Used to calculate revenue, average order value (AOV), and customer spending tiers. | Stored as string in raw due to formatting issues (e.g., currency symbols `$`, comma separators `,`), missing values (1.09%), and outliers (negative values `-150.00`, extreme values `99999.00`). | 1. Remove non-numeric characters (currency symbols `$`, commas `,`).<br>2. Cast to floating-point number.<br>3. Impute missing purchase amounts with the median value of the standardized product category, or drop.<br>4. Drop outlier rows (negative amounts, extreme amounts that represent system errors like 99999). |
| `shipping_country` | `Object` (String) | `Object` (String) | The destination country for the shipped order. | Used for geographic segmentation, shipping optimization, tax calculations, and localization strategies. | Inconsistent names/codes (e.g., "US", "USA", "United States", "UK", "U.K.", "United Kingdom"), and missing values (1.19%). | 1. Standardize country names to standard full country names (e.g., US/USA -> `United States`, UK/U.K. -> `United Kingdom`).<br>2. Impute missing countries using a default like `"Unknown"` or drop them if negligible. |

## Engineered Features (New Columns)

| Field Name | Type | Description | Business Relevance | Derivation Logic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `customer_age` | `Int64` | The age of the customer at the time of the transaction. | Helps understand the demographic distribution of purchasers and align product offers to specific age groups. | Derived as: `transaction_year` - `birth_year` (using parsed `transaction_date` and `date_of_birth`). |
| `email_domain` | `Object` (String) | The domain of the customer's email (e.g., `example.com`). | Used to segment customers (e.g., corporate domains vs. generic webmails like Gmail/Yahoo) for B2B/B2C targeting. | Extracted by splitting the standard `customer_email` at the `@` symbol. |
| `transaction_year` | `Int64` | The calendar year in which the transaction occurred. | Useful for annual financial rollups and multi-year comparisons. | Extracted from `transaction_date`. |
| `transaction_month` | `Int64` | The calendar month (1-12) of the transaction. | Used for monthly seasonality analysis and retail trend analysis. | Extracted from `transaction_date`. |
