TEST: Two-Sample Independent T-Test (US vs. UK Purchase Amounts)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Samples:
  - United States: N = 303, Mean = $656.66, Std Dev = $566.77
  - United Kingdom: N = 288, Mean = $626.97, Std Dev = $354.28
  - Observed Difference: $29.70

Hypotheses:
  - H0: Mean purchase amount in US equals UK (mu_US = mu_UK)
  - H1: Mean purchase amount in US differs from UK (mu_US != mu_UK)
  - Alpha: 0.05

Results:
  - T-Statistic: 0.7593
  - Degrees of Freedom: 589.00
  - P-Value: 0.4480
  - 95% CI of Difference: [$-46.27, $105.66]
  - Decision: Fail to Reject Null Hypothesis (H0)

Executive Takeaway:
No statistically significant difference detected (p = 0.4480 >= 0.05). The observed difference of $29.70 is within standard sampling variation. Business strategy can safely standardize pricing tiers, discounting models, and promotional campaigns across both the US and UK markets.
